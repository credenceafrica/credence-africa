# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior decision-makers across Africa's growth markets: institutional investors and fund
managers, ministers and public-sector leaders, founders and CEOs of SMEs and startups,
and the leadership of cooperatives, non-profits, faith-based organizations, and creative
and technology enterprises.

They arrive needing to do consequential things — raise capital, enter new markets,
navigate policy and public affairs, build leadership capability, convene stakeholders,
access intelligence, and strengthen strategic networks. They are time-poor, skeptical of
vendor hype, and judge institutional credibility within seconds. Their context is
evaluation: deciding whether Credence Africa is a serious enough partner to trust with
high-stakes mandates, usually before they ever book a consultation. The job to be done on
the site is simple — "convince me you can actually execute this, then make it easy to
start a conversation."

## Product Purpose

Credence Africa is a pan-African institution-building enterprise that integrates advisory,
capital raising, public affairs, executive education, convenings, intelligence, and
strategic networks into a single execution system for institutions shaping growth across
the continent.

The site exists to establish credibility, communicate the integrated operating model,
demonstrate depth across twelve sectors and seven service lines, and prove results through
real case studies and insights — and then convert qualified institutions into
consultations and mandates. Success is a senior decision-maker concluding "these people get
serious things done" and taking the next step: booking a consultation or engaging on a
mandate.

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative and decisive, with pan-African ambition.** Three words: *authoritative,
decisive, pan-African.*

Voice is expert confidence in plain, consequential language — "Building the institutions
that build Africa", "execution systems" — never hype, never jargon for its own sake. The
tone is that of a peer to ministers and fund managers, not a vendor pitching them. It
carries quiet power (we already do this work) and continental scale (this matters across
Africa, not one market). A visitor should finish a page feeling they are in genuinely
capable hands and sensing the ambition and reach of the work.

## Anti-references

This must NOT look like any of the following:

- **Generic Big-4 / management-consulting sameness.** Stock handshake and skyline photos,
  corporate blue, glassy gradients, vague "synergy / transformation / leverage" copy. The
  interchangeable advisory-firm look that signals nothing specific.
- **Flashy startup / SaaS.** Gradient-mesh heroes, playful illustrations, big rounded
  cards, emoji, growth-hacky energy. Reads as unserious to this audience.
- **NGO / aid-sector.** Charity-style beneficiary imagery, donation-CTA framing,
  development-jargon tone. Credence is an enterprise partner, not an aid recipient or
  donor.
- **Government / bureaucratic.** Dense, dated, form-heavy official-portal stiffness with no
  design point of view.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility before persuasion.** Earn trust with substance — real case studies, named
   outcomes, sector depth — before any selling. The work is the argument.
2. **Show the execution, don't claim it.** Concrete mandates, results, and the integrated
   model over adjectives. "Decisive" is demonstrated, never asserted.
3. **Institutional gravitas, not corporate sameness.** Feel like a serious, established
   partner without sliding into the interchangeable consulting-firm clichés above.
   Distinctiveness is part of the credibility.
4. **Continental in scale, specific in detail.** Express pan-African ambition through real
   markets, sectors, and stories — not generic "Africa rising" stock imagery or maps.
5. **Respect the reader's time.** Decision-makers scan. Lead with the consequential, keep
   the integrated model legible at a glance, and make the path to a consultation obvious on
   every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** on the public site: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (large/bold text
≥3:1), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and headings, and
a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Note the vermillion primary
(`hsl(10 83% 54%)`) is below 4.5:1 on white for small text — reserve it for large text,
buttons, and accents, and use the dark foreground for body copy.
